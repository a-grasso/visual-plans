## What changed

Implemented `EnrichmentRunner` end to end: it sequences the existing research and
extraction ports and persists one `AccountProfile` per account. One new endpoint
exposes it. The repository's `save` became an `upsert`.

<Callout tone="warn" title="Breaking contract change">

`ProfileRepository.save` was replaced by `upsert` (returns the persisted
profile). Any caller of `save` must migrate — grep confirms only the new runner
and one test used it.

</Callout>
